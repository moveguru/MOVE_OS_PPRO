# PPA Industry Day Requirements

Source: User-provided official notice text for `Personal Property Activity Information Technology Software Solutions Industry Day Synopsis and Registration`.

## Purpose

The PPA is hosting a focused Industry Day to engage with commercial partners regarding IT software solutions to modernize and increase the efficiency of processing military household goods moves.

## Primary objectives

1. Conduct market research on commercial software capabilities that support all aspects of IT software solutions specifically related to military household goods relocation services.
2. Software solutions include full platforms or IT software capabilities capable of integrating with a government-owned system.
3. Specific items of interest:
   - In Transit Visibility
   - counseling services
   - claims processing and adjudication
   - mobile applications
4. Provide equal and fair opportunity for registered participants to engage with a cross-functional team of PPA subject matter experts.
5. Conduct interactive demonstrations of relevant IT software solutions via individual meetings between the PPA cross-functional team and individual registered attendees.
6. Provide equal and fair opportunity for registered participants to provide written responses.

## Event details

- Interested attendees must register with the Contracting Officer.
- PPA will not allow registration after the window closes.
- After registration closes, registered participants receive event time, date, and schedule.
- Participants can withdraw if circumstances prevent attendance.
- On event day, PPA provides overview and detailed demonstration instructions.

## Written response

- All registered participants may submit an IT software solution overview.
- Must highlight company IT capabilities related to military household goods relocation services.
- Maximum: 10 pages.
- Font: 11 point.
- Submit to Contracting Officer.

## Demonstrations

- All registered participants may request opportunity to demonstrate.
- Demo request must be included in registration submission.
- Demonstrations subject to strict time limit based on number of requests.
- Time limit and schedule shared after registration closes.

## Registration

- Registration window: April 24, 2026 through May 8, 2026.
- Registration email: `[registration email in source notice]`.

Required information:

- Company name, attendee names and titles.
- Contact information for each attendee.
- Brief capability statement highlighting relevant IT software solutions.
- Whether company requests to demonstrate IT software solution capability to PPA.

## Disclaimer

- Notice is for information and planning only.
- It is not an RFP or promise to issue RFP.
- Government will not pay for information received or participation costs.
- Government may adjust event details including cancellation.

## Demo positioning implications for MoveGuru

MoveGuru / Master Surveyor should align directly to:

- mobile applications
- counseling services via AI-guided pre-move survey
- in-transit visibility via sticker/asset binding and future custody events
- claims/adjudication via evidence packet and digital manifest
- integration with government-owned systems via export packages and APIs/stubs
